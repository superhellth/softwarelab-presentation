1. Introduction

What system did we build, and why?

The idea

An AR "tour guide": visitors walk a real outdoor site, and content (3D models, sprites, spatialized audio stories) appears anchored to real-world GPS coordinates as they get close to a waypoint — no native app, no VPS, works straight from a QR code in the browser.

Motivation

  • Museum trails, campus tours, scavenger hunts, heritage-site guides
  • Zero-install: point phone camera at QR → AR opens directly in browser
  • No native SDK, no store review, works offline once loaded
  • Built on gps-plus-slam-app-framework — GPS↔AR sensor fusion that keeps 3D content pinned outdoors

Two roles, one tour

Authoring

Walk the site, drop waypoints at your live GPS position, attach a model/sprite + audio story, export a tour.zip.

Viewing

Visitor opens the tour link/QR, walks around, content prefetches at 25 m and appears at 10 m, taps to play the story.

2–3. Implementation details & learnings

Built one isolated, independently-runnable component at a time

Approach: components before composition

Each component lives under src/components/<name>/ with its own demo page and pure unit-tested core/ — no dependency on any sibling component. Composition into the two app modes (Authoring / Viewing) came last, not first.

The shared contract

Every component talks to one contract: tour.json schema + Redux slices, pinned in plans/Shared-Contract.md (decisions D1–D17) — agreed before splitting component work.

  • One Waypoint entity — "POI" is a rendering role, not a type
  • Proximity radii are per-waypoint fields; only the hysteresis margin is a global constant
  • Proximity zone machine is pure, GPS-free, world-space (X/Z) only
  • Asset provider: getAssetUrl(id) + release(id), ref-counted, one interface / three backings

Interesting problem: prefetch vs. jank

Zone transitions are monotonic single-step (never IDLE↔ACTIVE directly) — this lets the AR scene assume prefetch always precedes activation, so a knight model is already loaded by the time it needs to appear at 10 m.

🎥 demo recording: waypoint prefetch → activate transition

Interesting problem: replay-testable movement

Anything with a movement dependency (proximity, map, AR scene, authoring) is tested by replaying real recorded outdoor walks — deterministic tests on a desktop, no phone needed.
